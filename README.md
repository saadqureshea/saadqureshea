### Featured Projects

#### 🛒 [KAITO MarketPlace](https://github.com/saadqureshea/kaito-marketplace) · [Live Demo →](https://idyllic-lamington-6ac121.netlify.app/)

A full-stack digital marketplace and remote-work platform spanning four verticals: digital products, made-to-order goods, services, and job postings.

- **Payments** — Stripe Checkout with server-side price resolution (never trusts the client) and a commission engine that splits every sale 80/20, freezing the breakdown onto each order so historical records stay accurate
- **Role-based access** — five distinct roles (buyer, seller, worker, employer, admin), each with its own dashboard and route guards
- **Approval workflows** — listings and worker profiles move through `pending → approved/rejected` before going public
- **Extras** — reviews & ratings, file uploads, seller payouts, employer↔worker messaging, and multilingual English/Urdu UI with full RTL support
- **Tested** — unit coverage on the commission math and role-guard middleware, the two highest-risk paths

`React 18` `Vite` `Tailwind` `Node.js` `Express` `MongoDB` `Stripe` `JWT`

#### 🔄 [SkillSwap MVP](https://github.com/saadqureshea/skillswap-mvp)

A React Native mobile prototype letting students discover, offer, and book peer-to-peer skill-sharing sessions — browse skills, create offerings, book sessions, and manage a profile.

`React Native` `Expo` `JavaScript`
